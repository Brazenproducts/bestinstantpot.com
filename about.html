Who We Are

Best Instant Pot is an independent review site dedicated to helping you find the right pressure cooker, multi-cooker, or slow cooker for your kitchen. We're not owned by any manufacturer, we don't accept paid placements, and we don't let brands influence our rankings.

We're part of the What Are Best review network — a family of niche review sites that take the same honest, data-driven approach to product recommendations across multiple categories.

How We Rank Products

Every product on this site is evaluated using three core criteria:

  1. Amazon Review Score: We prioritize products with ratings of 4.5 or higher and at least 1,000 reviews. High ratings with high review counts indicate consistent quality across a large sample of real buyers — not just a handful of early adopters or incentivized reviewers.
  2. Return Rates: A product can have great reviews but still have quality issues. We factor in return rate data where available, because a low return rate means people are keeping and using the product — the ultimate vote of confidence.
  3. Price-to-Quality Ratio: The most expensive option isn't always the best. We evaluate what you actually get for your money, comparing features, build quality, and longevity against the price tag. A $80 pressure cooker that performs at 95% of a $250 model gets a higher ranking from us.

What We Don't Do

  • Accept paid placements: No brand can pay to be ranked higher or featured on our site.
  • Write fake reviews: Every review is based on real product data, user feedback, and hands-on experience.
  • Recommend products we wouldn't use: If we wouldn't put it in our own kitchen, it doesn't make our list.
  • Ignore problems: If a popular product has known issues, we'll tell you about them. Honest means honest.
