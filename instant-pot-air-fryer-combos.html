Best Instant Pot Air Fryer Combos for 2026

Best Instant Pot Air Fryer Combos for 2026

Pressure cook and air fry in one machine — here are the combos actually worth buying.

The idea is simple: pressure cook your food to make it tender and juicy, then switch to air fry mode to crisp the outside. It's brilliant for chicken wings, pork chops, roasts, and anything else that benefits from a crispy finish. Instead of two appliances hogging counter space, you get one that does both.

But not all combo units are created equal. Some air fry beautifully, others barely manage a light tan. We tested the top options and ranked them by our standard criteria — Amazon ratings, return rates, and real value for money.

1. Instant Pot Duo Crisp + Air Fryer — Best Overall Combo

Top Pick

Functions: 11-in-1 (Pressure Cook, Air Fry, Roast, Bake, Broil, Dehydrate + more)
Capacity: 6.5-quart | Price: $119–$149

The Duo Crisp is Instant Pot's answer to the Ninja Foodi, and it's a strong one. It comes with two lids — a pressure cooking lid and an air frying lid — which means each function gets dedicated hardware instead of compromising. The air frying results are genuinely crispy, not just "slightly less soggy."

The 11-in-1 functionality covers everything from pressure cooking and air frying to roasting, baking, broiling, and even dehydrating. The two-lid design does mean more storage, but it also means better performance in each mode. If you're already an Instant Pot fan and want to add air frying, this is the natural upgrade.

Build quality is solid Instant Pot standard, and the massive review count with a 4.6 rating tells you this thing delivers. Return rates are low, which is always a good sign for a multi-function appliance.

2. Ninja Foodi 9-in-1 — Best Air Frying Performance

Best Air Fry

Ninja Foodi 9-in-1 Deluxe (8-Quart)

★★★★★ 4.6 / 5 — 24,000+ reviews
Functions: 9-in-1 with TenderCrisp Technology
Capacity: 8-quart + 5-quart cook & crisp plate | Price: $149–$179

If air frying quality is your top priority, the Ninja Foodi edges out the competition. Ninja's TenderCrisp technology is specifically designed for the pressure-cook-then-crisp workflow, and it shows. The crisping results are consistently excellent — golden, crunchy exteriors with juicy interiors.

The 8-quart capacity is the largest on this list, making it ideal for families or batch cooking. The hinged crisping lid stays attached to the unit, so you won't lose it in a drawer somewhere. The downside is size — this thing is a beast. Measure your counter space before ordering.

Ninja's recipe ecosystem isn't as large as Instant Pot's, but it's growing fast. The included cook & crisp plate works well for air frying, and the overall build quality is excellent for the price.

Should You Get a Combo or Separate Units?

If counter space is limited (and whose isn't?), a combo unit makes a lot of sense. You get 80–90% of the performance of dedicated appliances in one footprint. The trade-off is that you can't pressure cook and air fry simultaneously, but that's rarely an issue in practice.

If you already own a standalone air fryer you love, there's no need to replace it with a combo. But if you're starting fresh or looking to consolidate, any of these three combos will serve you well. Our top pick is the Instant Pot Duo Crisp for its balance of performance, price, and the massive Instant Pot ecosystem behind it.
